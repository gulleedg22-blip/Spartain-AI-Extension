## 2. Who may use it

- If you are under 18, use Spartan AI only with the permission of a parent or guardian.
- Spartan AI does not contain an AI model. You connect it to one yourself: a model on your own computer, an Anthropic API key, or a team proxy someone shares with you. Each provider has its own terms and age rules, and you must follow them. Many providers, including Anthropic, require the account holder to be an adult. If you are under 18, the account or key must belong to a parent, guardian, or other adult who has agreed to let you use it.

## 3. Rules for using it

You agree to:

- Follow your teacher's instructions and your school's academic integrity policy. If a teacher says no AI on an assignment, do not use Spartan AI for it.
- Not hand in AI-written work as your own where that is not allowed.
- Not use Spartan AI during tests, quizzes, or exams unless the teacher clearly allows it.
- Not use it to harass, threaten, or harm anyone, or to do anything illegal.
- Not type or paste other people's private information into it.
- Not share a team passcode outside the group it was given to, and not try to extract or misuse anyone's API key.

You are responsible for what you do with the answers.

## 4. AI answers

AI answers can be wrong, out of date, biased, or made up. Read the AI Disclaimer, which is part of these terms.

## 5. Your information

What stays on your computer, in Chrome's storage for this extension:

- Your settings, including any API key or team passcode you enter.
- Your chat history (the most recent 200 messages).

What leaves your computer:

- **When you send a message**, your message and the earlier messages in that chat go to the AI provider you chose. If "Include page title and selection" is on, the title and address of the page you are viewing and any text you have selected on it go too. If you entered a display name, it is included.
- **Local model**: the message goes to software on your own computer and nowhere else.
- **Anthropic API key**: the message goes to Anthropic under that key's account.
- **Team proxy**: the message goes to a server run by whoever gave you the passcode, which forwards it to Anthropic. That person controls that server.
- **Update check**: about once an hour the extension asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. You can turn this off in Settings.

The maintainers do not run servers that collect your chats, do not use analytics, and do not sell data. They cannot see your settings, keys, or chats. The extension can read the title, address, and selected text of the page you are on; it does this to show you what would be sent and to send it when you ask.

Do not enter passwords, health details, home addresses, or anything else you would not want an AI company to receive.

- Keep keys and passcodes private. The maintainers are not responsible for charges on any key.
