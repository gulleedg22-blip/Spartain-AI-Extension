# AI Disclaimer

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Spartan AI shows text written by an artificial intelligence model. Read this before you rely on anything it says.

## AI can be wrong

- Answers can be inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, or biased.
- The AI can state false things with complete confidence. It can invent facts, quotes, sources, page numbers, and web addresses that do not exist.
- Summaries can leave out or change important points. Math and dates can be off.
- Citations from the "Cite this page" tool are a starting draft. Check every one against your teacher's required format and the real source.

**Check anything important against your textbook, your teacher, or another reliable source.**

## It is not a person or a professional

- Spartan AI is not a teacher, tutor, counselor, doctor, or lawyer. Do not use it for medical, mental health, legal, or safety decisions.
- It is not for emergencies. If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. If you are struggling, talk to a parent, a counselor, or another adult you trust, or call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the United States).

## Your schoolwork is your responsibility

- Using AI on an assignment may break your teacher's rules or the school's academic integrity policy. Ask first if you are not sure.
- You are responsible for everything you turn in, including any part an AI helped with.
- Learning the material is the point. An answer you do not understand will not help you on a test.

## The schedule can be wrong too

The bell schedule and day types in Spartan AI come from documents the school published and, for later dates, from a student's own calendar. Days marked as estimates are guesses. The school's official calendar and announcements always win.

## What the AI sees

When you send a message, the AI provider you chose receives your message, the earlier messages in the chat, and, if page context is on, the title, address, and any selected text of the page you are viewing. Do not send anything private. The Terms of Use explain this in full.

## Not a school product

Spartan AI is a student project. It is not made, reviewed, or endorsed by De La Salle High School, and the answers it gives do not represent the school.
